Major aspects

Calculated in-browser from orbital elements (JPL/Standish) and a truncated lunar theory (Meeus). Planet accuracy is about 1′ to 2′ and the Moon is within about 0.1°. That's fine for sign and degree placement, not for precise timing work. Nakshatras & padas use the selected ayanamsa (Lahiri, or Krishnamurti in KP mode). In KP mode each placement also shows its sign lord · star lord · sub lord. Houses use the whole-sign system and need your birth city. City data: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). North Node shown is the true node.
